More about the holiday home

A historical landmark

The house is built on Nodeberget, which is considered a historical landmark in Skålvik on the idyllic Flekkerøya, just outside Kristiansand. The modern building, built in 2024, is located on a hill with panoramic views of the sea where you can see the boat connection to Kristiansand City. There is a driveway right to the house, with 2 parking spaces and a boat mooring. It is a short walk to the shop on Flekkerøya (which you pass on the way to the house), and about 15 minutes' drive to Kristiansand City centre.

Outdoor areas

Sheltered and sunny

  • In front of the house there is a sheltered and sunny patio with lawn, with panoramic views of the sea.
  • The outdoor areas have a pergola for pleasant seating comfort.
  • Outdoor shed (towards the parking lot) is used to store a gas grill for outdoor grilling and outdoor chairs and cushions.
  • Outdoor shed (towards the sea) is used for fishing equipment and garden tools. The fishing equipment can be freely used if desired.
  • It is possible to rent kayaks and paddleboards.
  • There is a private boat mooring about 100 meters from the house with the possibility of renting a boat. There are fine fishing conditions in the sea right outside the house with good fishing opportunities (including cod, saithe, pollack, mackerel).
  • The plot borders a beautiful, public swimming area with deep water and rocks. This place can be used together with other permanent residents and cottage guests.
Swimming facilities

Swimming spot 40 meters from the house

There is a great swimming spot 40 meters from the house. With its beautiful rocky shores, the swimming spot is the perfect place to enjoy hot summer days and take a refreshing swim for both children and adults.

There is a natural staircase up from the sea as you plunge into the fresh water, and a nice, small beach for small children.

Boating and fishing opportunities

Try your luck fishing!

There is a lot of fish around Flekkerøya, especially mackerel, cod, saithe and plaice. You can fish both from land or from a boat. Complete fishing equipment is available in the house.

The holiday home has its own boat mooring. Please contact us if you would like to rent a boat.

There are also excellent conditions for kayaking – there are two kayaks for rent.

Outdoor areas

Unique sea views

Around the house there are beautiful outdoor areas, with unique sea views. There is access to good, sunny seating areas, with a pergola and gas fireplace for outdoor grilling, beautiful terraces and lawn. There are also two outdoor sheds attached to the house.

Parking

Two parking spaces with EV charging

The holiday home has 2 parking spaces in a private parking lot right next door. Park as close to the stairs to the holiday home as possible. One parking space has an electric car charging station.

It is also possible to park more cars than the 2 in the parking lot, see directions for other parking lots – these are located about 700 meters away, near Asperøyveien.
