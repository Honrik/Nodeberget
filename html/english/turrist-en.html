Tour guide and experiences

Art, culture and nature

In Kristiansand and the surrounding area there are many opportunities for experiences, art and culture. You can visit the Animal Park, the Water Park (Aquarama), Kunstsiloen, the Climbing Center in Kristiansand or the Cannon Museum at Møvik. Or how about a trip to the charming Fiskebrygga in Kristiansand, with its great selection of fish and shellfish?

On Flekkerøya there are beautiful outdoor areas with small, idyllic coves, beaches/bathing areas and hiking trails for public use. Flekkerøya is known for its unique biodiversity, with lush vegetation and unique species.

Furthermore, Flekkerøya has been a military strategic location throughout history, and there are many war monuments, especially from World War II, which are easily accessible around the island (and not far from the cabin).

See visitsorlandet.com for more and updated activity offers.

Dyreparken and Badeland Sørlandssenteret · 26 km from the house · $$$

Norway's largest adventure park with a zoo with animals from all over the world – from large predators and colorful birds to domestic animals and small reptiles. Inside the Zoo there is a huge water park, with separate tickets. In addition, there are many activities, entertainment and shows.

Odderøya Kristiansand · 14 km from the house · $

Odderøya is an island in the Kristiansandsfjord, connected to the mainland by bridges south of the city center, and is establishing itself as a regional cultural center with artists, galleries and filmmakers in the old barracks. On the island there are many interesting traces of military activity – from the Great Northern War up to and including the Cold War. Important traces from the fighting on April 9, 1940 are visible and partly marked with information signs.

Visit the website →
The Cannon Museum Møvik (before the Flekkerøy tunnel) · 6.1 km from the house · $

In beautiful hiking terrain at Møvik outside Kristiansand is a cannon battery from World War II. The cannon at Møvik is the world's second largest cannon mounted on land. The battery was started in 1941 and, together with the cannon battery in Hanstholm in Denmark, was to control shipping traffic in the Skagerrak. The cannons had a range of 55 kilometers.

Visit the website →
Bragdøya Bragdøya · 11 km from the house · $

Bragdøya is a municipal open space in the heart of Kristiansand's city fjord – a typical southern archipelago island, slightly hilly with knolls and peaks, sheltered depressions and patches of land that provide room for a diverse range of plant and animal life. You will find fine sandy beaches and sheltered rocky cliffs perfect for sunbathing and swimming. On Bragdøya you can encounter wild sheep all year round.

Culture and festivals

Summer in Kristiansand

Kristiansand has many festivals and concerts throughout the year, especially during the summer period. There are annual events such as Sommerbris, Måkeskrik, Palmesus and Ravnedalen Live – or you can visit the cultural center in Kristiansand, with all its activities.

Palmesus is a music festival held at Bystranda in Kristiansand city center every summer. Palmesus is described as Scandinavia's biggest beach party with over 20,000 guests and top artists from all over the world. Palmesus focuses on young people.
