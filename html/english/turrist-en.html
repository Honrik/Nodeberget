In the area

Tourist Attractions

The Kristiansand area has plenty to offer beyond the sea rocks — experiences, festivals and training for the whole family.

Experiences

Outings for the whole family

On Flekkerøya, there are beautiful outdoor areas with small, idyllic coves, beaches, and hiking trails for public use. We recommend visiting Dyreparken or Badeland, the Climbing Centre in Kristiansand, Kanonmuseum at Møvik, or the unique Michelin-starred restaurant under the water, "Under", located at Lindesnes. Or how about a trip to the charming Fiskebrygga in Kristiansand, with its delicacies from the sea.

Festivals

Summer in Kristiansand

Kristiansand has many festivals and concerts throughout the year, especially in the summer. Here you will find Sommerbris, Måkeskrik, Palmesus, Ravnedalen Live, or you can visit the cultural center in Kristiansand, with all its activities.

Sommerbris Måkeskrik Palmesus Ravnedalen Live

Sports & Training

Stay in shape on holiday

There is a separate training arena at Flekkerøy, only 2 kilometers from the holiday home. Furthermore, there are several gyms in the area, including Crossfit Vågsbygd and the fitness center Aquarama Kristiansand.
