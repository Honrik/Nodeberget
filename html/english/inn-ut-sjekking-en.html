Check-in

Normal check-in at the holiday home is at 12:00 on the day of arrival, according to Airbnb guidelines. If you would like a different check-in time, this can be arranged.

Before check-in, you will be sent an electronic key code so that you can check in yourself.

Check-out

Normal check-out is at 12:00 on the last day. If you wish to check out at a different time, this must be agreed upon. Upon check-out, the following must be completed:

  • The holiday home must be cleaned.
  • The dishwasher must be emptied, and cutlery/service must be put in place.
  • The tables and kitchen counter must be cleared/wiped.
  • All rubbish and empty items must be emptied/removed – and rubbish must be wrapped in bags and thrown into rubbish bins. You can find rubbish bins in the parking lot right next door – during the winter period, this must be thrown at the cabin waste collection point at the turnoff at the end of Skibbuveien, Skålevig.
  • If you have used towels – these must be washed and put back in order.
  • The grill is cleaned (if it has been used).
  • Kayaks and paddle boards are put away.
  • Report any damage or things that are not working.
