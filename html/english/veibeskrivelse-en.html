How to find us

Directions

Årekjerret 93 is located about a 20 min. drive from Kristiansand center.

  1. Drive E39 out of Kristiansand towards Stavanger, then towards Vågsbygd. Follow national road 456 towards Vågsbygd for about 6.7 km.
  2. Drive through the tunnel to Flekkerøya – about 2.3 km long.
  3. At the roundabout at Flekkerøya, take the 3rd exit to Skålevik. Follow this road as far as you go – that is, until it narrows. At the very end of the road, turn left to Årekjerret.
  4. Follow Årekjerret as far as it goes and turn right up a small hill. You will then come to a private parking lot. Park your car near the garage, towards the stairs up to Årekjerret 93.
  5. Årekjerret 93 is the house that is "directly above" the garage on the left.
