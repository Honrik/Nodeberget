The practical part

Practical Info

Everything you need to know before and during your stay at Nodeberget.

Main door

The main door has an electronic key lock with a code. The code must be used by opening/ locking the door. Note: When pulling through, the door must be held / locked so that it does not slam shut.

Sliding doors

The sliding doors are opened / closed by pushing the handle to the right / against the wall with both hands. I.e. one hand on the door and one hand on the handle. Then turn the knob to the left. Then the door opens. NOTE! Make sure the handle is pointing down when using the door!

Gas fireplaces

The gas fireplaces are started/closed by their own remote control. The gas for the gas fireplaces is placed outside between the outdoor shed and the cabin towards the mountain / parking lot in a closet. There is also a gas fireplace that can be used for barbecues outside.

Internet

There is internet in the cabin. Network name and password will be assigned at check-in.

Music / Sonos

There are integrated Sonos speakers on the 1st floor of the holiday home that can be accessed via the Internet.

TV

It's a large smart TV that can be accessed via Chromecast.

Garbage

All rubbish must be wrapped in bags – and disposed of in trash cans in the parking lot. The key to these bins is in the holiday home.

Summer period

During the summer period, rubbish is thrown into containers at the parking lot. It is recycling:

  • Paper/cardboard in "green" containers
  • Other waste in "grey" containers
  • Wine bottles and glasses are thrown into separate containers

November to April

During the winter period, all rubbish must be disposed of in containers placed at the cabin waste collection point at the turning point at the end of Skibbuveien, Skålevig. The same source sorting applies. This is located on the road midway between the cottage and the roundabout (Kiwi shop). Skibbuveien is located on the old main pier at Flekkerøy, down to the sea (about 200 meters from the main road).

How to find us

Directions

Årekjerret 93 is about 20 minutes' drive from Kristiansand city center.

  1. Drive on E39 from Kristiansand towards Vågsbygd (towards Stavanger from Kristiansand). Follow Highway 456 towards Vågsbygd for about 6.7 km.
  2. Drive through the tunnel to Flekkerøya – approximately 2.3 km long.
  3. At the roundabout on Flekkerøya, take the 3rd exit to Skålevik. Follow this road as far as it goes – when it narrows down at the very end, turn left to Årekjerret.
  4. Follow Årekjerret as far as it goes and turn right up a small hill. You'll arrive at a private parking lot. Park your car near the garage, close to the stairs leading up to Årekjerret 93. You can also park in front of the garage. Årekjerret 93 is the cabin located "directly above" the garage on the left side.

Check in

Normal check-in at the holiday home is at 14:00 on the day of arrival, according to procedures in Airbnb. If you would like a different check-in time, this can be arranged. After the agreement is finished, you will be sent an electronic key code so that you can check in yourself.

Check out

Normal check-out is at 14:00 on the last day. At a different check-out time, this must be arranged. At check-out, the following must be done:

  • The holiday home must be cleared
  • The dishwasher must be emptied, and cutlery/service must be put in place
  • The tables and kitchen counter must be cleared/dried off
  • All rubbish and empty goods must be emptied/removed – and rubbish must be wrapped in bags and disposed of in waste containers. Rubbish containers can be found in the parking lot close by.
  • If you have used towels – these must be washed and cleaned back
  • The grill is cleaned (if it has been used)
  • Kayaks and Paddle Boards are laid in place
