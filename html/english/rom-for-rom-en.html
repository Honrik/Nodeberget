The leisure home

Room by Room

Get to know the holiday home – room by room, from the entrance area to the blue bedroom.

Level 1

1st Floor

  • Large hallway and cloakroom
  • Large, open living room with large windows, smart TV and gas fireplace
  • Large, open kitchen with all amenities
  • Access to terrace with fantastic panoramic sea views
  • A large bedroom with double bed
  • Bathroom with toilet & shower
  • Laundry room, including washing machine, dryer and freezer
  • Technical storage room and wine room (not available to guests)
The entrance area

A spacious welcome

The holiday home opens with a spacious entrance hall, with its own walk-in wardrobe for outerwear, suitcases, shoes and life jackets.

In the wardrobe room there is a key cabinet for keys to the sheds and boat, as well as a display for handling alarms and fires. Access to the key cabinet must be clarified with the owner. In the event of an alarm in the home, the same code as the door code must be used to end the alarm.

If the alarm goes off, the home must be inspected to clarify what triggered the alarm. See the Contact Info page to report the alarm.

Living room and kitchen

Access to terrace and lawn

The holiday home has a large and spacious living room and kitchen. The living room contains a spacious sofa group with space for 8 people, with a smart TV, a dining table with space for 10 people and an open kitchen. The airy room extends over 2 floors, where the ceiling is more than 6 meters high. The room faces the sea, with floor-to-ceiling windows.

The kitchen consists of modern appliances including a stove, dishwasher and refrigerator. There is a Nespresso coffee machine, knives and various kitchen utensils. There is a separate kitchen island with plates and cutlery for more than 20 people.

Bedroom on the 1st floor

The yellow bedroom

The bedroom has a nice and large double bed with bedside tables and a wardrobe for clothes. The window faces the outdoor area with pergola and storage room.

There are integrated connectors (USB and USB-C) for convenient charging of PC/mobile phones.

Bathroom on the 1st floor

Modern and heated

Modern bathroom with toilet, sink and shower, with a separate heated clothes/swimwear hanger and heated rack.

Laundry room and storage

All the practical things in place

Large laundry room with washing machine, dryer and freezer. The room also serves as a storage room for various cleaning equipment such as vacuum cleaners and various laundry equipment. There are also several shelves and cabinets for pillows, duvets and bedding.

A cot and high chair are also stored in this room. Various games, playthings and card games are stored here.

In addition, there are two other rooms; a technical room and a wine room (not available to guests).

Level 2

2nd Floor

  • Living room with gas fireplace and access to terrace with fantastic panoramic sea views
  • Three large bedrooms with double beds
  • Two large bathrooms with toilet and shower. One bathroom has a bathtub
  • Walk-in closet (not available to guests)
Fireplace living room

Access to terrace

The fireplace living room has two comfortable wingback chairs in front of the gas fireplace, with a view of the sea. There are sliding doors to the terrace.

The fireplace living room has an open layout, with lots of windows and light, and is well suited for relaxing, e.g. reading a book, having a glass of wine and enjoying the view and the peace.

Bedroom on the 2nd floor

The green bedroom

The bedroom has a nice and large double bed with bedside tables and wardrobe for clothes. The window faces the outdoor area with pergola and storage room.

There are integrated sockets (USB and USB-C) for charging PC/mobile phones.

Bedroom on the 2nd floor

The red bedroom

The bedroom has a nice and large double bed with bedside tables and wardrobe for clothes. There is a window facing the outdoor area with pergola and storage room.

Here there are integrated connectors (USB and USB-C) for charging PC/mobile phones.

Bathroom on the 2nd floor

Large and modern

Here you will find a large and modern bathroom with toilet, sink and shower.

Master Bedroom on the 2nd floor

The blue bedroom

The bedroom has a large, airy guest area with a large double bed, bedside tables and wardrobe for clothes. There are large floor-to-ceiling windows facing the sea. In the morning, the morning sun comes into the room.

Integrated sockets (USB and USB-C) for charging PC/mobile phones.

Master bathroom

With bathtub

The bedroom also has immediate access to a large and modern bathroom with toilet, sink, shower and bathtub.

The walk-in closet is closed off for guests.
